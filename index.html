The Craft

The Art of Clarification

A highly precise four-step ritual transforming organic milk into pure liquid gold.

01

Carefully Selected

Premium quality organic milk is hand-selected from cows on open green pastures during peak spring grazing season.

02

Traditionally Cultured

Milk is slowly fermented using natural starters to develop signature buttery aroma, complex organic acids, and micro-nutrients.

03

Slowly Clarified

Pure cultured butter is simmered in small brass and copper vats, allowing water to evaporate and milk solids to toast gently.

04

Sealed at Peak Freshness

Prisinted ghee is strained through double filters and filled directly into pre-heated glass jars to lock in the warm scent.
